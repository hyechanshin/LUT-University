1.     Choose a Software System
Select any software system, product, or service that interests you. It can be something you use often like an app, website, or platform.

2.     Analyze Sustainability Impacts Using the SusAF Tool
Use the Sustainability Awareness Framework (SusAF) tool to identify potential sustainability impacts of your chosen system.

o   Consider all five dimensions of sustainability: technical, environmental, social, individual, and economic.

o   Reflect on the three orders of impact (direct, indirect, and systemic).

o   Identify at least three sustainability impacts (positive and negative).

3.     Create a SusAD Diagram
Develop a Sustainability Awareness Diagram (SusAD) to visually illustrate the chain of effects.

o   You can use the sample SusAD diagram provided below as inspiration.

4.     Write a Short Reflection
Write a short summary explaining how the negative impacts you identified could be reduced or mitigated.
