1. Explain the difference between theory testing and theory creation research.

Theory testing is a deductive process and is closely related to quantitative research. This can be a survey, unit testing, or it supports a theory to be backed up by facts and tests. While theory creation is an inductive process that uses qualitative tools to develop concepts and solutions to real-world problems, it can take the form of case studies, analyses, etc. Therefore, these two, theory testing and theory creation, are closely related to each other, supporting the lifecycle.


2. What is your opinion, how is positivism related to theory testing and creation?

Positivism focuses on factual data and is based on quantitative research. Finding a pattern, survey, unit testing, lab test, and also pursuing a large amount of data.
