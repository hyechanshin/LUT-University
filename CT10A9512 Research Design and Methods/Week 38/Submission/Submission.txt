1. How would you classify grounded theory in Orlikowski & Baroudi's classification? Is it positivist, interpretive or critical approach? Why?

Grounded theory is highly interpretive, as it uses participant-generated data and real-world experiences. However, grounded theory can also adopt a positivist or critical stance depending on how the researcher applies it.

2. Give an example of how ethnography could be used in software engineering research.
For instance, studying how developers work and interact in an IT company, particularly how they work using Agile methods.
