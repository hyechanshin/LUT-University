1	Why is the narrow meaning of science problematic for software engineering?

Generally, science seeks knowledge about the natural world, which can clash with software engineering. For instance, in this rapidly evolving era of technology, new challenges arise, and new findings are added to our existing knowledge. Predefined methods can be outdated, while software can contain bugs and unexpected results based on the software environment and the hardware being used.
